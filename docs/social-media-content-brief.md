None of this means "don't post exciting content" — it means ground the excitement in what's

## 3. The games — what they are and how to play them

There are three games live today: **BlackRed**, **Heritage**, and **Caged**. Each has its own
rhythm, so they suit different content formats — use the "best for" notes to match a game to
a post idea.

### 3.1 BlackRed — card colour prediction

**One-line hook:** Predict red or black, card by card — the more you call, the bigger the
swing.

**How to play, step by step:**

1. Choose how many cards to predict — from 1 up to 5 in a row.
2. For each card in that sequence, call its colour: black or red.
3. The game shows the potential return for that exact prediction *before* you confirm your
   stake — nothing is hidden until after you've committed.
4. Confirm your stake and reveal the cards.
5. All of the cards in your sequence have to match your prediction for that round to pay out.
   The more cards you call (up to 5), the bigger the potential return shown — and the harder
   it is to get every single one right.

**Best for:** quick-hit posts, "how far would you push it" polls (1 card vs. 5 cards),
before/after reveal clips.

**Content angles:**

- "1 card or 5? Comment your call before you see the odds."
- The reveal moment itself — card flip animations are naturally short-form-video friendly.
- Simple explainer carousels for new players: "This is how BlackRed works, in 4 slides."

### 3.2 Heritage — Nigerian royal regalia, instant win

**One-line hook:** Pick your royal, pick five tiles, reveal the regalia.

**How to play, step by step:**

1. Choose a **tradition** — one of seven Nigerian royal traditions (full list in §4).
2. Choose **King or Queen** for that tradition.
3. The board has nine tiles, face-down. Pick any five of them (or use Quick Pick to have five
   chosen for you — same odds either way, it just changes which tiles you personally touched).
4. Confirm your stake.
5. All nine tiles reveal. Some are "winning" positions; how many of *your* five chosen tiles
   land on a winning position decides your prize tier — the best outcome (jackpot) is when
   all five of your picks are winning positions. When that happens, you see the **complete,
   correct five-piece regalia set** for the tradition and leader you chose: headpiece, neck
   piece, torso garment, hand piece, and footwear, all from the same royal outfit, not a mix
   of different traditions.

**Best for:** cultural-pride content, "which royal would you pick" engagement posts,
educational content about Nigerian traditions, the big reveal moment as a payoff clip.

**Content angles:**

- "Which tradition's regalia hits different? Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa–Fulani, Edo, Efik–Ibibio,
  Ijaw, or Middle Belt — tell us below." (See §4 for the full names to use accurately.)
- A weekly "spotlight" rotating through each of the seven traditions with its real regalia
  names and meanings (§4 gives you the exact names — don't improvise beyond them).
  King vs Queen comparison posts for the same tradition.
- The complete five-piece reveal as the payoff shot for a short video.

### 3.3 Caged — live rising-multiplier game

**One-line hook:** Watch it climb. Cash out before it drops.

**How to play, step by step:**

1. During the betting window before a round starts, place a stake — you can actually place
   **up to two separate stakes** in the same round if you want two shots at different cash-out
   moments.
2. Once the round starts, a multiplier starts climbing live, in real time.
3. For each stake you placed, you decide when to cash out — the longer you wait, the higher
   the multiplier (and your potential return) climbs, but it can crash at any moment.
4. Cash out before the crash and you lock in whatever the multiplier was at that instant.
5. If a stake isn't cashed out before the round crashes, that stake is lost for that round.
   A new round and a new betting window start right after.

**Best for:** tension/suspense clips, live-reaction style content, "did they cash out in
time" short-form video — this is the most naturally watchable game because the climb and the
crash are both visual, real-time moments.

**Content angles:**

- Slow-motion or replay-style clips of a close cash-out call.
- "Early cash-out vs. greedy cash-out" — which instinct do you trust?
- Two-stake strategy content: cash one out early, let the other ride.

### 4.2 The regalia, by tradition

Each tradition's king and queen have their own complete five-piece regalia set — headpiece,
neck piece, torso garment, hand piece, and footwear. These are the real local names used in
the game:

**Yoruba** — King: *Adé* (beaded crown), *Ìlẹ̀kẹ̀* (coral beads), *Agbádá* (robe),
*Ìrùkẹ̀rẹ̀* (flywhisk), *Bàtà* (shoes). Queen: *Gèlè* (headwrap), *Ìlẹ̀kẹ̀* (beads), *Ìró àti
Bùbá* (blouse and wrapper), *Abẹ́bẹ́* (fan), *Bàtà* (shoes).

**Igbo** — King: *Okpu Eze* (royal cap), *Ákà* (coral beads), *Isiagụ* (lion-patterned
tunic), *Mkpọ* (carved staff), *Akpụkpọ ụkwụ* (shoes). Queen: *Okpu ákà* (beaded headpiece),
*Ákà* (beads), *Akwa* (blouse and wrapper), *Àkùpè* (feather fan), *Akpụkpọ ụkwụ* (shoes).

**Hausa–Fulani** — King: *Rawani* (ceremonial turban), *Abin wuya* (neck panel), *Babbar
riga da alkyabba* (robe and cloak), *Sanda* (staff), *Takalmi* (slippers). Queen: *Ɗankwali*
(headwrap and veil), *Abin wuya* (gold necklace), *Riga* (gown), *Mafici* (fan), *Takalmi*
(shoes).

**Edo (Benin)** — King: *Erhu Ivie* (coral crown, also called *Ede*), *Ododo* (coral neck
collar), *Ewu Ivie* (coral-mesh tunic), *Ukhurhe* (staff), *Ibata Ivie* (coral slippers).
Queen: *Ukpe-okhue* (coral headdress, also *Okuku*), *Ivie* (coral beads), *Ukpon-Ododo*
(ceremonial attire), *Ezuzu* (fan), *Ibata Ivie* (slippers).

**Efik–Ibibio** — King: *Itam* (feathered cap, also called the Ntinya-crowned cap), *Nkwa*

**Ijaw** — King: *Ogbogbo* (coral-trimmed hat, also *Bolou*), *Ila* (coral beads), *Etibo and

**Middle Belt** — King: *Agbande-Tsa* (woven cap), *Akaa* (coral/traditional beads), *A'nger*

staff), *Akov a Anger* (A'nger-upholstered shoes). Queen: *Ichoho-Tsa* (headtie), *Akaa*
(beads), *A'nger* (paired with a *Riga* lace blouse), *Pepe* (carved wooden fan), *Akov a
Anger* (slippers).

### 4.3 Content ideas built from this table

- **"Pick your royal" weekly series** — rotate through the seven traditions, one per week,
  pairing the king and queen regalia side by side.
- **"Name that regalia" quiz-style posts** — show the artwork, ask followers to guess the
  local name, reveal the answer with its meaning.
- **King vs. Queen split-screen** for a single tradition — visually comparable, easy carousel
  format.
- **"Which footwear is yours"** or similar single-item spotlight — the shoes/slippers across
  traditions (*Bàtà*, *Ikpa Ukot*, *Angala-gbasa*, etc.) are a fun, low-stakes comparison.

### 4.4 Handle with care

Only use the names and descriptions above. Don't:

- Claim a specific living traditional ruler wears or endorses this regalia.
- Describe any item as sacred, forbidden, or spiritually powerful beyond generic "royal
  tradition" framing — some real regalia (coral beads especially) carries genuine cultural
  weight, and the product's own content has already been through review to avoid
  overstepping; new captions shouldn't reintroduce that risk by improvising further.
- Mix items from different traditions into one "fantasy" outfit — part of the respect here is
  keeping each tradition's regalia internally accurate.

## 5. Voice and tone

Betplus's own copy is short, declarative, and confident without being hypey. Real examples

- "Find your game."
- "Know your balance."
- "Set your limits."
- "Choose your game, make your call and watch it unfold."

## 9. Glossary — terms worth knowing before writing copy

- **Stake** — the amount a player risks on a round.
- **Return / payout** — what a player gets back if their round wins; shown before confirming
  a stake on BlackRed and Heritage.
- **Prize tier** — on Heritage, the level of win based on how many of your five picks landed
  on winning positions; jackpot is the top tier (all five).
- **Reveal** — the moment a round's outcome is shown (BlackRed's cards, Heritage's tiles).
- **Cash out** — on Caged, locking in the current multiplier before the round crashes.
- **Round** — one complete play cycle of a game, start to settlement.
- **Wallet** — where a player's funds live; deposits and settled winnings are tracked
  separately.
- **Self-exclusion** — a player-initiated break from all Betplus games for a chosen period.
