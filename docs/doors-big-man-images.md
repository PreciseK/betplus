# Big Man popup artwork

The supplied Big Man reference now replaces the procedural Blender host in the win popup. The vault compartments and treasure remain live 3D. The existing Blender sources are retained as earlier assets, but are not loaded for the popup.

Saved images in `apps/web/public/assets/doors/`:

- `big-man-reference.png`: original user-supplied white-background image.
- `big-man-cutout.png`: transparent standing pose.
- `big-man-thumbs-up.png`: transparent congratulatory pose, used by the win popup.

Both edits used the built-in image generation tool with `transparent_background: true`. Original files were preserved. PNG alpha was checked after generation.

## Background removal prompt

Edit the supplied image only to remove the white background. Deliver the same full-body Big Man character isolated on a fully transparent alpha background. Preserve his exact face, expression, sunglasses, slicked hair, body proportions, frontal standing pose, hands, rings, shoes, black-and-gold embroidered jacket and waistcoat, white shirt, chains, and all existing colours and details. Do not redesign, restyle, change pose, crop feet, add shadows/background or text. Keep clean natural edges and preserve the white shirt and pocket square as opaque parts of the character. No white halo. Output a transparent PNG cutout.

## Thumbs-up prompt

Create an additional pose variant of the exact Big Man character in the reference. Preserve his identity, slick dark hair, dark gold-rim aviator sunglasses, stocky muscular build, gold chains, black and ornate gold floral embroidered suit jacket and waistcoat, open white shirt, black trousers, gold jewellery, and polished black shoes. Full body standing facing viewer. Change his right arm (on viewer's left) to a clear confident THUMBS UP, hand held around chest-to-shoulder height, four fingers naturally curled and thumb clearly extended upward. His other arm rests naturally at his side. Give him a warm, confident closed-mouth smile appropriate to congratulating a player. Same polished realistic 3D character style as reference, no redesign. Entire body including shoes and raised hand visible with comfortable transparent margins. Isolated character only, truly transparent alpha background, no floor, no shadows outside character, no text or props. Carefully formed anatomically correct hand with one thumb and four curled fingers.
