**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SWITCHER OVERVIEW**

This document outlines the four selectable operating models for the admin console to manage house margin, player retention, and insolvency risk across our game engines (BlackRed, BirdEscape, and Heritage). It establishes strict parameters for mathematical integrity, explains why raw forced-loss (M14) is replaced by the legally compliant Daily Loss-Stop (M15), and details game engine compatibility.

**3\. Critical Technical & Regulatory Analysis: 'Force Model' Implementation**

**REGULATORY COMPLIANCE WARNING: THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN REFUSING AND RIGGING**

Operators frequently request a 'Force Model' to guarantee that the house never suffers a losing day. However, there is a fundamental dividing line between a legally viable risk-control mechanism and criminal fraud:  
<br/>1\. Rigged Forced-Loss (Illegal M14): Accepting a player's bet on the premise of fair chance and then dynamically forcing an outcome to lose because the house is in drawdown constitutes cheating under LSLGA gaming statutes and obtaining money by false pretenses. Crucially, RoundLifecycleService publishes commitmentDigest before betting opens. A rigged outcome cannot match the cryptographic hash, giving tech-savvy players definitive mathematical proof of fraud.  
<br/>2\. Daily Loss-Stop via Bet Throttling (Legal M15): Operators retain the unfettered legal right to refuse wagers, lower table limits, or cap high-multiplier offerings. When the daily net loss limit is reached, Model 3 stops accepting high-liability wagers, protecting bankroll solvency while ensuring 100% cryptographic integrity for every wager accepted.

**5\. Admin Architecture: Zero-Downtime State Machine**

To enable seamless admin switching without interrupting live sessions or breaking cryptographic hashes, the engine utilizes a state-transition queue governed by the following operational rules:  
<br/>1\. Active Round Immutability: When an admin switches the active model (e.g., from Model 1 to Model 3), the change does NOT apply to rounds currently in progress. Any round whose commitmentDigest has been broadcast must settle under its originating model parameters.  
2\. Next-Round Transition Queue: The model change is staged in Redis cache under \`engine:pending_model_switch\`. At the completion of the settlement phase (\`RoundLifecycleService::settleRound\`), the engine reloads the active configuration.  
3\. Audit Logging & Regulatory Export: Every model switch is timestamped with Admin ID, Previous Model, New Model, Justification Reason, and live Float Balance. These audit logs are exportable directly for LSLGA compliance inspections.
